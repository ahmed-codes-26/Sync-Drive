Drive Sync

A lightweight background tool that automatically syncs a folder on your computer to your own Google Drive.

Read the Privacy Policy →

What it does

Simple, automatic file sync

👁

Watches a local folder

Point Drive Sync at any folder on your computer and it keeps an eye on it for changes.

☁️

Uploads new & changed files

When files are added or modified, they're uploaded to your own Google Drive automatically.

🔇

Runs quietly in the background

Set it up once and forget about it. Drive Sync works silently without getting in your way.

🔄

Catches up on missed changes

Turned your computer off? Drive Sync picks up where it left off and syncs anything it missed.

How it works

Three steps, then it's hands-free

1

Sign in with Google

Authenticate once through Google's secure sign-in flow. Drive Sync only requests access to files it creates.

2

Choose a local folder

Pick the folder on your computer that you want to keep synced to Drive.

3

Files sync automatically

Your files are uploaded to a dedicated folder in your Google Drive — no manual steps required.

Your data stays yours

Built with privacy in mind

  • Drive Sync runs entirely on your own computer — there is no server or database operated by me.
  • Files go directly from your computer to your own Google Drive. No middleman.
  • Your Google OAuth token is stored locally on your machine and never leaves it.
  • You can revoke access at any time from your Google account.
